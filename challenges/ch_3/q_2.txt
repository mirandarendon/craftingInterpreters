question:
This informal introduction leaves a lot unspecified. List several 
open questions you have about the language’s syntax and semantics. 
What do you think the answers should be?

answer:
- why does 1 + 1 print 2, not 2.0, if Lox only has one numeric type 
(double). My guess: the book's stringify() just strips a trailing .0 
for display, but internally everything is still a double.
- why does redeclaring a variable with var work at the global scope but 
throw a compile-time error inside a block, if both are just var 
declarations? My guess: globals are treated more loosely (maybe to make 
the REPL forgiving when you re-run a line), while locals are checked 
statically by the resolver so shadowing bugs get caught early.
